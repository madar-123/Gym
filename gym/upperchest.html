Upper chest Workouts

Tips Instructions Target Muscles
Incline Dumbbell Press: This exercise is a great way to target your upper chest muscles. Lie on an incline bench with your feet flat on the floor. Hold dumbbells in each hand with your palms facing each other. Lower the dumbbells down to your chest, then press them back up to the starting position.
Incline Barbell Press: Similar to the incline dumbbell press, this exercise uses a barbell to target the upper chest. Lie on an incline bench with your feet flat on the floor. Hold the barbell with an overhand grip, slightly wider than shoulder width. Lower the barbell down to your chest, then press it back up to the starting position.

* Keep core tight and avoid twisting for better isolation.
* Keep your core engaged throughout the exercise.
* Don't lock your elbows at the top of the movement.
* Focus on controlling the dumbbells throughout the range of motion.
